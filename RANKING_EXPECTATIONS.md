**Priority: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ CRITICAL**

- Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
- Create/claim your business listing
- Add complete information:
  - Business name: "Locksmith TA"
  - Address: Your Atlanta address
  - Phone: (678) 650-4802
  - Hours: 24/7
  - Services: List all services
  - Photos: Add 10+ photos
- **Why**: This is 60% of your ranking! Without it, you won't rank well.

### **2. Get Customer Reviews**
**Priority: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ CRITICAL**

- Ask satisfied customers to leave Google reviews
- Aim for 20+ reviews in first 3 months
- Respond to all reviews (positive and negative)
- **Why**: Reviews are huge ranking factor + build trust

### **3. Submit Sitemap to Google**
**Priority: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ HIGH**

- Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
- Add your website
- Submit sitemap: `https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml`
- **Why**: Helps Google find and index all your pages

### **4. Build Citations**
**Priority: ⭐⭐⭐ MEDIUM**

- List on Yelp (free)
- List on Yellow Pages (free)
- List on Angie's List
- List on Better Business Bureau
- **Why**: Builds online presence and trust
